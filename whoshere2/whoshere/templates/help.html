1. Reading the board

Open the Board{% if board_address %} at {{ board_address }}{% endif %} in any web browser, on a phone or a computer. It updates itself every 30 seconds.

The banner at the top answers one question: is everyone gone?

All clear to arm

No tracked staff are on campus. It's safe to arm the alarm.

Not clear — 2 on campus

Someone is still here. Their names and where they are appear right under the heading.

Can't confirm — check manually

The board hasn't heard from the Wi-Fi system for a few minutes, so it won't guess. Walk the building before arming.

Each person has a status

HereTheir phone is on the Wi-Fi now.
Here?Their phone dropped off the Wi-Fi in the last {{ grace_min }} minutes. They're probably leaving, or their phone is just asleep. They still count as on campus until the {{ grace_min }} minutes are up.
Not hereGone. The board shows when they left.

Where and when

  • Where is the area of the building whose Wi-Fi their phone is using, like Sound Booth or Gym. It's the nearest Wi-Fi point, so think "around there" rather than an exact room.{% if show_wifi %} The small grey label next to it is the Wi-Fi network they're on.{% endif %}
  • When shows since (when they arrived) for people who are here, and left for people who aren't.
  • Not tracked under the banner lists staff with no phone set up. The board can't see them, so it can't vouch for them.
  • Click anyone's name to see their comings and goings (see History).

2. Before you arm the alarm

  1. Open the Board{% if chat_on %}, or type /allclear in Google Chat{% endif %}.
  2. Green "All clear to arm": go ahead and arm.
  3. Red "Not clear": check the names.
    • People marked Here? are probably on their way out. Wait a few minutes and look again.
    • People marked Here are still in the building. Find them, or give them a call.
  4. Orange "Can't confirm": walk the building as you would without the board, and let an administrator know.
  5. Glance at Not tracked: those people won't show up on the board even if they're here.

3. Looking back: History

The History tab lists every arrival and departure, newest first, grouped by day.

  • Click a person's name, on the Board or in History, to see just their history for the last 30 days, along with where they are right now.
  • Use the first drop-down to pick one person, or leave it on Everyone.
  • Use the second to choose how far back to look: the last day, week, month, 90 days or year.
  • Arrived shows where they first connected. Left shows where they were last seen.

A departure is recorded at the time the phone was last seen, not {{ grace_min }} minutes later when the board decides they've gone.

5. Google Chat

You can ask Who's Here from Google Chat, on your computer or in the Gmail or Google Chat app on your phone.

Start a chat with it (once)

  1. Open Google Chat: chat.google.com, the Chat section of Gmail, or the Google Chat app.
  2. Click New chat, type Who's Here, and choose the app with the maroon person icon.
  3. Click Start chat. From then on it's in your list of chats.

Then type one of these

/whoshereWho's on campus and where.
/whoshere ThomasLook someone up by name: where they are, or when they were last here.
/allclearIs it safe to arm the alarm?
/historyToday's arrivals and departures. Add a name for one person.

In a chat with Who's Here you can also just type plain words: all clear?, a name, or help. If you use it in a team space, only you see its answers.

Questions

I'm at church but the board says I'm not here.
Check that Wi-Fi is on and you're connected to the staff network, not the guest one or cellular only. If you are, Private Wi-Fi Address may have been turned back on, which can happen after a phone update. Turn it off again (see Your phone).
I left an hour ago and I'm still showing as here.
Another device of yours may still be on the Wi-Fi, such as a laptop or tablet left in the building. Or someone else's phone may have been added under your name. Let an administrator know.
Why does it say "Here?" with a question mark?
Phones drop off Wi-Fi when they go to sleep. The board waits {{ grace_min }} minutes before deciding you've left, and shows "Here?" while it waits.
Is it tracking me everywhere?
No. It only knows which church Wi-Fi point your phone is connected to while you're on the church network. It can't see anything when you're away, and it never sees what you do on your phone.
The banner says "Can't confirm".
The board couldn't reach the Wi-Fi system for a few minutes. Treat the board as unknown and check the building yourself. An administrator can see the reason on the Status page.

For administrators

Sign in with Admin at the top right. Then:

  • People: add, rename or hide staff. Hidden people disappear from the board.
  • Devices: every phone the Wi-Fi has seen. Pick a person and click Assign. Phones whose name matches a person are suggested.
  • Locations: give Wi-Fi points friendlier names for the board.
  • Status: whether the board is getting data, and why not if it isn't.
  • Backup: export or import everything, for moving to a new server.
  • Google Chat: connection status and the service account key.

Installation and setup are in the project's README on GitHub. See Revision history for what changed in each version.
