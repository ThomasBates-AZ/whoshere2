Apple Watch

The Who's Here watch app shows the board on your wrist and can tap you when it's all clear to arm. Notifications go out through Apple's push service, so the server only makes outgoing connections to Apple; nothing has to be opened to the internet. Setup steps are in the README under "Apple Watch".
