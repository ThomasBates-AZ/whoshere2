Before you arm the alarm

  1. Open the board{% if chat_on %}, or type /allclear in Google Chat{% endif %}.
  2. Green: arm it.
  3. Red: people marked Here? are likely on their way out, so check again in a few minutes. Anyone marked Here is still inside.
  4. Orange: walk the building.
  5. Check the Not tracked names too.

Looking back: History

Click anyone's name on the board to see their arrivals and departures. The History tab shows everyone; pick a person and a time range (day, week, month…) from the drop-downs. Departures show the time the phone was last seen.

Something looks wrong?

  • Here but shown as not here: check Wi-Fi is on, you're on the staff network, and Private Wi-Fi Address is still off. Phone updates can switch it back on.
  • Left but still shown as here: a laptop or tablet of yours may still be on the Wi-Fi. Tell an administrator.

Start a chat with Who's Here (one time)

  1. Open Google Chat: chat.google.com, the Chat section of Gmail, or the Google Chat app on your phone.
  2. Click New chat, then type Who's Here.
  3. Pick the app with the maroon person icon , then click Start chat.

It now sits in your chat list like any conversation. Open it whenever you want to ask.

Using it in a team space? Click the space's name → Apps & integrations → Add apps → Who's Here. Only the person who asks sees the answer.

Type a command

/whoshereWho's on campus, and where.
/whoshere ThomasLook someone up by name: where they are, or when they were last here. Part of a name works.
/allclearIs it safe to arm the alarm? Answers all clear, who's still here, or "can't confirm".
/historyToday's arrivals and departures. Add a name for one person: /history Sam

Type / in the message box to see the list. In your chat with Who's Here you can also type plain words: all clear?, a name, or help.
