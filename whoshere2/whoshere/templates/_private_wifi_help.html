iPhone

  1. Open Settings → Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap the ⓘ next to {{ staff_wifi }}.
  3. Tap Private Wi-Fi Address and choose Off. On older iOS versions, turn the switch off.
  4. Tap Continue if your iPhone asks to rejoin the network.

Android

Menus differ a little between phone makers.

Google Pixel and most Android phones

  1. Open Settings → Network & internet → Internet (or Wi-Fi).
  2. Tap the gear ⚙ next to {{ staff_wifi }}.
  3. Tap Privacy and choose Use device MAC.

Samsung Galaxy

  1. Open Settings → Connections → Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap the gear ⚙ next to {{ staff_wifi }}, then View more.
  3. Tap MAC address type and choose Phone MAC.

Your phone reconnects to the Wi-Fi afterwards. If it doesn't, turn Wi-Fi off and back on. This change only affects {{ staff_wifi }}, not other networks you join.
